1)Intro:
A Gane without Commerical Game Engines like Godot or Unity. 
Languages - C ++ 
Avanti Savoia is a 2D FPS shooter set in the Austro-Italian front during the WW1 from the POV of an Ardito-Fiamme Nere.

2)Controls:
W -> Move forward
S -> Move backward
Right Shift + (W/S) -> Move faster forward / backward
When not in melee mode :
    left click -> Fire rifle
    right click -> To enter melee mode
When you get in melee mode : 
    left click -> Attack with dagger (dagger causes more damage)
    right click -> Escape melee mode
    
3)Melee Mechanic:
There's a colour scheme in place of art to help distinguish between different phases/ stages/ modes of the player (ardito):
    a) player holding a rifle (normal mode) -> green
    b) player in melee and holding a dagger (melee mode) -> blue
    c) player plunging his dagger (attack) -> red  
To enter melee mode, right click. Henceforth, your left clicks will be registered as commands to plunge the dagger and momentarily you'll be red before going back to being blue. Right click again to enter back to normal mode and use you your rifle
There's also a speed buff to the player (ardito) if he's in melee mode. ;)
